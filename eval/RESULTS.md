## Notes on the one miss
In `13_unemployment_overpayment` (used photo), the right edge of the page was cut off in the photo, so the sentence ending "NO LATER THAN 12/21/2026" wasn't visible. The model worked out "30 days after the mail date" itself and got 12/20. **PlainSpeak's date check (`checks.date_written_in`) keeps a date like that out of the countdown and the calendar**, because it isn't written in what the AI read. A rerun of the same photo left the date blank and warned that the photo was cut off.

## What we changed because of this evaluation
- The first run showed the AI **calculating** deadlines from phrases like "within 14 days after service" (eviction notice, court summons). Those dates depend on when the person received the letter, so showing them as definite is risky. We added a rule to the prompt and, more importantly, a check in code: only dates actually written in the letter go into the countdown and calendar. Other deadlines are shown as a time period with a "count from the day you got the letter, and call to confirm" warning.
- The first run also showed that the larger `gemini-3.8-flash` model took about 37 s per letter. We switched to `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`: about 3 s per letter, with the same accuracy on this test set.
